How I Learn

I treat certifications as checkpoints, not the whole story. A course can teach me a tool or concept, but I usually understand it better once I try to build something with it.

That's why a lot of my recent learning has happened through personal projects — experimenting with n8n, local AI models, APIs, web development, databases, and AI-assisted coding while working through problems as they come up.

Building gives me a different kind of feedback: something either works, breaks, behaves unexpectedly, or needs to be redesigned. That's usually where the real learning starts.

What I'm Exploring Now

I'm exploring how AI can be used as part of the development process — not just for generating content, but for prototyping, debugging, reasoning through problems, and turning ideas into working software.
